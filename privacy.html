Privacy Policy

Privacy-first EV range planning.

UPerifery is designed to work without accounts, profiles, or unnecessary personal data collection.

Effective date: July 25, 2026

Short version: UPerifery does not require login, account creation, or a cloud profile. Location and vehicle values are used to calculate range behavior inside the app experience.

Information We Use

UPerifery may use information you enter or allow in the app to calculate EV range estimates, including vehicle parameters, battery level, calibration values, selected drive mode, temperature assumptions, terrain/condition values, and start location.

Location Data

If you use current location, iOS may provide your device location so UPerifery can center the map and calculate the reachable perimeter. You can also enter a custom address or location manually. Location permission is controlled by iOS Settings.

No Account System

UPerifery does not require an account, login, password, authentication system, or cloud profile.

Purchases

UPerifery may offer a one-time lifetime Pro unlock through Apple In-App Purchase. Purchase processing, receipts, refunds, family sharing eligibility, and restore behavior are handled by Apple according to Apple’s terms and systems.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not use the app to build advertising profiles. Information may be processed by Apple services such as StoreKit, MapKit, or system location services when you use related features.

Support Emails

If you contact support, your email address and message contents are used to respond to your request. Avoid sending sensitive information that is not needed for troubleshooting.

Children’s Privacy

UPerifery is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can address the request.

Accuracy Notice

Range estimates are informational only. Real driving range can change due to weather, terrain, road closures, traffic, speed, payload, battery condition, tires, and vehicle behavior. Always rely on safe driving judgment and your vehicle’s actual systems.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app changes. Updates will be reflected by changing the effective date on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact ikanev@icloud.com.
