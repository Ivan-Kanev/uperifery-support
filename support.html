Why does the heatmap change after applying settings?

UPerifery recalculates the perimeter from the latest battery, consumption, weather, reserve, terrain, vehicle, and calibration values. Advanced settings are staged first, then applied together to avoid unnecessary recalculation.

Are EV presets exact?

No. Presets are reference starting points based on public-style vehicle assumptions. Real range depends on trim, wheels, tire pressure, temperature, speed, elevation, payload, and battery condition.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the Pro screen and use Restore Purchases. The app uses Apple’s standard purchase flow and does not require an account.

Does UPerifery replace navigation?

No. UPerifery is a range visualization and planning aid. Always use real navigation, road conditions, charging information, and your vehicle dashboard before driving.
